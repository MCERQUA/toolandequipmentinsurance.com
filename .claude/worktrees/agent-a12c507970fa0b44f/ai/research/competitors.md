# Competitor Landscape — Tools & Equipment Insurance

## Direct / adjacent (contractor & equipment insurance specialists)
- National online insurers (Next Insurance, Hiscox, biBERK, CoverWallet, Thimble) — broad,
  generic small-business contractor coverage; often write blanket tool coverage at actual cash
  value with per-item caps and lack inland marine nuance.
- Equipment-specific specialists (Hiscox inland marine, Travelers inland marine, Zurich) writing
  scheduled floaters but usually through captive or large-agency channels.
- Regional contractor brokers in high-theft markets (TX, Northeast, Gulf).

## Our wedge (why gear-heavy contractors switch to CCA)
1. **Scheduled tools at replacement cost** — competitors default to blanket ACV with per-item
   caps that pay $150 for a $600 nailer. We schedule gear and pay replacement cost.
2. **The GL-to-tools gap, explained up front** — most contractors don't know GL excludes their
   tools until after a theft. We build the floater that closes the gap.
3. **Mobile equipment at agreed value** — lifts, compressors, and welders get undervalued on
   generic policies. We schedule each unit at agreed value.
4. **Equipment breakdown included** — internal failures (blown compressor, fried generator) that
   property excludes; competitors routinely leave this gap.
5. **Trades-native broker** — former contractor on staff; no explaining a tool inventory or a
   jobsite theft. Inland marine is a specialty class most generic agents don't understand.
